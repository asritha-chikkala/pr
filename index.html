📄 The Paper: Overview

Bayesian Networks for Lung Cancer Detection

A retrospective study on 9,940 patients from Southern Denmark (2009–2018), comparing Bayesian Network models against machine learning for early lung cancer triage in primary care.

Study Design Summary

Southern Denmark Cohort (2009–2018)
  • ✓
    9,940 patients from Southern Denmark (2009–2018): Comprehensive real-world primary care cohort.
  • ✓
    High-risk population: Patients referred by general practitioners with suspected lung cancer.
  • ✓
    23 clinical and laboratory features: Demographics, smoking status, and 20 routine blood biomarkers.
  • ✓
    Bayesian Networks vs. DES (ML) comparison: Head-to-head evaluation against Discriminative Ensemble Selection.

Key Findings

Empirical Results from Henriksen et al.
  • ✓
    BN models robust to 30% missing data: Maintained stable discrimination across missing levels.
  • ✓
    AUC: 0.737–0.757: Strong diagnostic discrimination matching complex ensemble ML algorithms.
  • ✓
    Clinical utility above 5% risk: Decision curve analysis demonstrated clear net benefit.
  • ✓
    Interpretable decision-making: Transparent probabilistic graph allows clinician auditing.
